# Business glossary

Terms Business (Sales/Solutions/Account) uses day-to-day. Written for Product and Tech to read
when they need to understand what Business means, not for Business's own reference.

## Business-process terms

- **OEE** — Overall Equipment Effectiveness. The one number the customer cares about; Business
  talks in "OEE went up/down," not in Availability/Performance/Quality separately.
- **The one-pager / GTM line** — the single sentence shown to a prospect or used in a renewal
  conversation. Currently: "see real-time OEE for any line, and automatically generate a
  corrective work order the moment OEE drops below target." (See `knowledge-base-business-use-case`.)
- **Renewal conversation** — a conversation with an existing customer about continuing or
  expanding the contract. The write-capability promise originated there.
- **Q4 business review** — the internal meeting where Business has to show this feature is
  working. Expect this capability to be referenced there.
- **Customer-facing promise** — anything said out loud to the customer, whether or not it has
  been built. Distinct from what Product has scoped or Tech has built — that gap is the main
  conflict here.
- **Differentiator** — what makes this product win over a competitor's. Here: "an assistant
  that acts," not just another dashboard.
- **Kickoff** — the point work is agreed to start. Business does not always know at kickoff
  whether its promise matches what Product and Tech have scoped.

## Plant-floor / customer-domain terms

Business has to understand these to sell into a plant, even though Business doesn't do this
work directly.

- **Shift** — operators work fixed shifts; OEE is almost always reported per shift.
- **Changeover** — switching a line from one product grade to another. Planned, not an
  Availability loss.
- **Unplanned stop / breakdown** — an unexpected equipment failure. Counted as Availability
  loss.
- **Scrap / reject** — product that fails quality spec. Feeds the Quality component.
- **Root cause** — the actual underlying reason for a stop or quality miss, found by
  maintenance after the fact.
- **MTBF / MTTR** — Mean Time Between Failures / Mean Time To Repair. Standard maintenance
  KPIs customers use; not currently referenced in any of these documents.
