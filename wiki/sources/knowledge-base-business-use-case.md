# OEE Chat Assistant — Business use case

The business-facing artifact for the `knowledge-base` epic (Veridion Chemicals, Calder Valley
Plant). States the customer-facing promise Sales has used: "see real-time OEE for any line,
and automatically generate a corrective work order the moment OEE drops below target."
Includes a worked example (OEE and loss drivers for Production Line 3) and the business
rationale (differentiator against dashboard-only competitors; came up in the Veridion renewal
conversation).
